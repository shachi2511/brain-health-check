INSPECTION CHECKLIST
1. License: the current state license is displayed at the front counter.
2. Inventory: physical counts are reconciled with the tracking system every month.
3. Lab results: every batch on the shelf must have a passing lab test result dated within the last 18 months.
4. Labels: every package shows batch number and test date.
5. Security cameras: footage is kept for at least 60 days.
6. Staff training: new hires complete compliance training within their first 30 days.
